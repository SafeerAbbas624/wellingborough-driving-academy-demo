Same street. Same honest approach.

Wellingborough Driving Academy opened at 45 Broad Green in 1977 and has been teaching the town to drive ever since. No gimmicks, no mis-selling and no price rises, just patient instruction from qualified instructors.

We are WADDIA members and work to the DVSA ADI Code of Practice. If anything ever concerns you, speak to your instructor, then the proprietor, Dave. The DVSA’s instructor conduct team is there if it still isn’t resolved.
