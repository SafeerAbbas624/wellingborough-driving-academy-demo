Since 1977, on Broad Green

We opened our doors at 45 Broad Green in 1977, and we have been teaching Wellingborough to drive ever since.

We are the only school in town with its own classroom, so new and nervous pupils can learn what happens before they ever pull away. Theory coaching is free for anyone who asks, whether you learn with us or not.

No gimmicks, no mis-selling and no price rises. Just honest, patient instruction at a fair price.

We run as close to carbon neutral as we can: an electric car on the fleet, more than 80% of our waste recycled, and charity weeks where 10% of the week’s profit goes to a good cause.

If anything ever worries you, talk to your instructor first, then to me. If it still isn’t right, the DVSA’s instructor conduct team will look at it.
